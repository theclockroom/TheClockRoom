时钟的房间与宏观认知债务记账框架

——AI、金融与算法平台的映射链分析

The Clock Room and the Macro-Cognitive Accounting Framework:
A Mapping-Chain Analysis of AI, Finance, and Algorithmic Platforms

五卷书 · 中英双语 · 创作者罗晟 Luo Sheng

核心概念与框架由作者独立创设 · 部分中文写作与完整英文翻译由 AI 辅助
Core concepts and frameworks are the author’s original creation · part of the Chinese text and the full English translation AI-assisted

设想一个没有窗户的房间，里面挂着 N 个时钟。每个钟都在走，都在给出读数。门外有人问：现在几点？ 房间里的人能读出每个钟的时间，却无法回答——他不知道任何一个钟与外部真实之间的映射关系。 他拥有读数，却没有校准。

Imagine a windowless room with N clocks on the wall. Every clock is running; every clock gives a reading. Someone outside asks: what time is it? The person inside can read every clock, yet cannot answer — for he knows the mapping between none of the clocks and the world outside. He has readings, but no calibration.

——全书从这个思想实验出发，把诊断推向现代社会最依赖的三类超长映射链：金融市场、算法平台与人工智能。
— From this thought experiment, the book carries the diagnosis into the three kinds of ultra-long mapping chains that modern society depends on most: financial markets, algorithmic platforms, and artificial intelligence.
