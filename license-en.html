Statement on Creation

The core concepts, theoretical frameworks, and key terminology of this book are the original creation of the author, Luo Sheng, including: the Clock Room thought experiment; the Macro-Cognitive Accounting Framework (MCAF); the fiber framework and mapping-chain analysis; three-layer irreversibility; the pseudo-small fiber; meta-audit; the media conductivity coefficient; the cognitive Reynolds number and its Fermi estimation (including the laminar–turbulent flow classification); the narrative leverage ratio; functional-recursion and pathological-recursion analysis of mapping-chain systems; monopoly over the mapping function; the Discrete Leviathan; and algorithmic xiangyuan. These analytical frameworks and tools are applied to the cross-domain analysis of three classes of ultra-long mapping-chain systems: finance, artificial intelligence, and algorithmic platforms.
